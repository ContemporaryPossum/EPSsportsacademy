Why EPS Football

More than drills. A love of the game.

Our football programme follows a common core curriculum designed around child development and physical ability. Each level builds on the last, adding new skills and new challenges as your child grows.

The syllabus was developed in Brazil and is refined with input from sporting experts and experienced coaches. Every head coach holds at least a Sports Science degree, and classes are kept small so every child gets attention.

Class levels

Four levels. One clear pathway.

Not sure where your child fits? A trial class lets our coaches assess them and recommend the right group.

2–4YRS

Soccer Toddlers

Simple, playful games that teach running, group play and kicking — building balance and coordination. Parents join in.

45 min weekly Parent involvement
5–6YRS

Soccer Kiddies

Ball control, kicking and the basics of a match — understanding two teams and learning to pass.

1 hr weekly
7–12YRS

Soccer Junior

Advanced ball control, fitness, passing and player positions, with more match play every week.

1 hr weekly
U6–U14

Competitive

For committed players: skills, fitness and game knowledge. Teams represent the academy in city tournaments and leagues.

1 hr, twice weekly Tournaments
