The EPS difference

Fun on the surface. Serious coaching underneath.

EPS started in 2013 with one idea: age-appropriate sport that blends fun and play with real child-development principles. Our goal is simple — to be the gold standard for kids' sports programmes in Malaysia.

Team EPS!
  1. 01

    Sports-Science head coaches

    Every head coach holds at least a Sports Science degree, and every coach is qualified and professionally trained.

  2. 02

    Small classes, big attention

    Baby and children's classes are kept deliberately small, so every child is seen, encouraged and coached.

  3. 03

    Structured progression

    Clear levels from toddler classes to competitive squads, so families can track — and celebrate — real progress.

  4. 04

    A syllabus with Brazilian roots

    Coaching syllabi developed in Brazil and kept up to date with input from sporting experts and experienced coaches.

Player pathway

From first kick to match day.

Each level builds on the one before. Scroll to follow the journey.

  1. 2–4YRS

    Soccer Toddlers

    Running, kicking and group games that build balance and coordination — with a parent alongside.

    45 min · weekly
  2. 5–6YRS

    Soccer Kiddies

    Ball control, kicking and first matches: two teams, passing and the basics of the game.

    1 hr · weekly
  3. 7–12YRS

    Soccer Junior

    Advanced ball control, passing, fitness and player positions — with more match play.

    1 hr · weekly
  4. U6–U14

    Competitive

    For committed players. Skills, fitness and game knowledge — representing EPS in city tournaments and leagues.

    1 hr · twice weekly
  1. 3–5YRS

    Water Kiddies

    Water safety, floating, paddling and lots of water confidence.

    1 hr · weekly
  2. 6–12YRS

    Water Junior

    Safe entries, front and back floats, and travelling up to 10 m.

    1 hr · weekly
  3. 25m

    Intermediate

    Coordinated breathing across strokes and 25 m in a stroke of choice.

    1 hr · weekly
  4. 400m

    Pre-competitive

    400 m continuous swimming and technique in all four strokes.

    1–2× weekly
  5. 800m

    Competitive

    Stamina for 800 m, plus a 100 m individual medley with legal turns.

    1–3× weekly
