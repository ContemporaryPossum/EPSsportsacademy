What happens at camp

Never a dull minute.

Every day mixes energetic play with hands-on, creative activities — and new camp activities keep returning campers excited.

Sports galore

A taste of lots of sports, coached the EPS way — with plenty of running, laughing and cheering.

Arts & crafts

Hands-on creative projects campers are proud to take home.

Team-building games

Challenges that teach kids to listen, share, and win (and lose) together.

Water fun

Water sports and splashy games to cool off in the Malaysian heat.

Healthy eating

Campers learn about healthy snack and lunch choices — and make some themselves.

Kitchen science

Fizzing, foaming experiments (volcano foam, anyone?) that turn curiosity into giggles.
