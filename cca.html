Street Dance

EPS Street Dance · Ages 3–16

Street Dance

Dancers of all levels learn popping, locking and hip hop. Classes are creative, blending hip hop styles with modern commercial moves.

1-hour weekly class All levels 10-week term plan

A term at a glance

  1. Hip hop basics IMoving to the beat with the two-step and the bounce.
  2. Hip hop basics IIHip hop poses and a first set of choreography.
  3. Hip hop basics IIIToprock with legs and hands, plus a short routine.
  4. Party moves IOld-school moves like the Running Man, the Prep and the Smurf.
  5. Party moves IIA short routine using the party moves.
  6. ChoreographyPutting the past weeks together in one routine.
  7. FreestyleTurning the steps into their own freestyle.
  8. CypherEveryone gets a turn to show off their freestyle.
  9. Final routine ILearning the term's final choreography.
  10. Final routine IIPolishing and performing the final choreography.

The plan can be made shorter or longer to suit the term.

EPS Ballet · Ages 3–16

Ballet

For young dancers learning the basics: positions of the arms and feet, basic ballet steps and how to combine them into graceful sequences.

1-hour weekly class All levels 10-week term plan

A term at a glance

  1. Introduction to balletParallel and heel movements; pointing and flexing the toes.
  2. First & second positionsPlié, grand plié, dégagé and battement tendu.
  3. Third to fifth positionsBarre exercises with and without music.
  4. Arm positionsAdding arm and head movements to barre work.
  5. Grand battementsBarre exercises with grand battements.
  6. JetésSimple exercises for jetés.
  7. Relevé & rond de jambeBarre work with music, plus centre practice.
  8. Fondu & développéWith head and arm alignment at the barre.
  9. FrappéShort frappé exercises.
  10. PirouetteLearning to spot, with half turns at the barre.

FAQ

Good questions, quick answers.

Can't find what you need? WhatsApp us

What ages and levels do you coach?
Football, basketball, swimming, street dance and ballet are open to ages 3–16 at every skill level. Multisport is for ages 3–6.
How long are classes?
Every CCA class is one hour, once a week.
How are children grouped?
By age first, then by ability after a coach assessment — so every child is challenged at the right level.
How many children per coach?
We keep set ratios: 6 children per coach for Water Kiddies and Multisport, 8 for our youngest football and basketball groups and junior swimmers, and up to 10–15 for senior groups.
Will I see my child's progress?
Yes. Every activity follows term and class plans, and each child receives an assessment and feedback form at the end of the term.
How do payments work?
You register through our GymMaster member portal. A registration fee applies, then fees are debited monthly from your credit card (set up for 12 months). You can join on any date — the first month is pro-rated.
Can I cancel or pause?
Yes, with 30 days' notice by email. A pause (freeze) lasts up to 30 days, can be requested twice in 12 months, and carries a fee per request.
Can siblings share one account?
Yes — add them as "linked members" in the GymMaster app, or ask us and we'll set it up for you.
